Vehicle Entry Management System

VEMS Privacy Policy
Last Updated: August 13, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to VEMS (Vehicle Entry Management System). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users, including guards, campus administrators, vehicle owners, and visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, processes, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

To provide a secure and streamlined vehicle tracking and authorization service, we collect the following categories of data:

  • User Account Details: Name, phone number, department, student/employee year (for vehicle owners and guards).
  • Vehicle Information: License plate/registration number, vehicle make, model, type, and color.
  • Device Camera & OCR Data: The app utilizes the device camera (used by gate guards) to scan license plates via Google ML Kit Text Recognition. This OCR processing is done entirely on-device; video feeds are never streamed or uploaded to any third-party servers.
  • Entry/Exit Logs: Timestamps of vehicle entry and exit events, gate identifiers, and verifying guard information.

How We Use Your Information

The collected data is strictly used for the following operational purposes:

  • To verify and authorize vehicle access to restricted campus gates.
  • To keep a secure, real-time log of vehicles currently present on the premises.
  • To send notification updates regarding vehicle status and entry verification.
  • To allow administrators to audit traffic flow and address security incidents.

Note: We do not sell, lease, or distribute your personal or vehicle log data to any third-party advertising companies or marketing networks.

Data Storage & Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All user session tokens and credentials are encrypted and stored securely on-device using Flutter Secure Storage. Server-side log data is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

Device Permissions Required

The VEMS mobile application requires the following device permissions to operate:

  • Camera: Required exclusively for the License Plate Scanning module to run Optical Character Recognition (OCR) locally.
  • Storage: Required if uploading registration files, vehicle images, or RC documents.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy page here. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates.
